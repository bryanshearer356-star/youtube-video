How this works

This is an unofficial fan tool. It is not made or endorsed by the game's publisher.

Card data. Cards released after that date are missing, and balance changes since then are not reflected.

The win rate. Each deck is played through hundreds of simulated battles against six other strong decks built from the same cards. It tells you how decks compare with each other. It does not predict your real arena win rate, because real opponents use different cards.

Offense and defense. On offense your deck moves first and is played carefully, the way a person would. On defense it moves second and is played the way the computer plays: it picks cards at random and only combines when it happens to be able to. Defense decks therefore favour cards that are strong without help.

What the simulator assumes. The battle rules are rebuilt from the in-game skill descriptions and community notes, so treat results as a strong guide, not a guarantee.

  • Five cards in hand, five slots in play, one card played per turn.
  • A card or combo waits one turn before it attacks.
  • Combo strength follows the community formula based on both cards' levels. Combo mastery is not included.
  • Hero levels are assumed to be maxed, and every hero is assumed to be available.
  • The attacker loses if the battle reaches 50 rounds.

If a recommended deck plays badly in the real game, tell Claude what happened and the rules can be corrected.
